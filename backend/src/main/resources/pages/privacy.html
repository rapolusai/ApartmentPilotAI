ApartmentCare privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026 · applies to the ApartmentCare Android app and its server

ApartmentCare helps a residential apartment association manage maintenance dues, expenses, notices, bookings and residents' requests. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, who can see it and how to have it removed. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Google Play's user data policy.

What we collect

InformationWhy it is needed
Name, mobile number, optional email, flat number and whether you are an owner or tenantTo identify you to your apartment's Admin and to sign you in. Your PIN is stored only as a one-way hash.
Maintenance bills, payment declarations, receipts and payment proofs you uploadTo keep the apartment's accounts and give you receipts.
Notices, poll votes, bookings, complaints and help requests you create or respond toTo run the apartment's day-to-day operations.
Vehicle registration numbers entered for your flatFor parking and gate management by the apartment.
Technical records: sign-in attempts, the time of each request and an identifier of the device's network addressTo protect accounts against PIN guessing and to investigate problems. These records are kept for a short period and then removed automatically.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos other than the files you choose to upload, or any advertising identifier. There are no advertisements and no analytics or tracking libraries.

Who can see your information

  • You see your own records and the apartment's shared information.
  • Your apartment's Admin and Treasurer see residents' details, payments and requests for their own apartment only, because they run the association's accounts and verify payments.
  • Other residents see shared notices, polls, public reports with apartment totals and the resident directory their Admin chooses to share. They never see your payment proofs or private requests.
  • The service operator (the developer of ApartmentCare) can access the server to keep it running, fix faults and act on deletion requests. We do not sell or share personal information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Information is stored on a server in India. It is sent only over encrypted connections.

How long we keep it

Your account details are kept while your account is active. Financial records (bills, payments, receipts, expenses and the audit trail behind them) are kept by the apartment association for as long as it is required to keep its accounts, because they are the association's records, not only yours.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: More → your profile → Delete my account, then confirm with your PIN.
  • Without the app: use the account deletion page. We verify the request before acting on it.

When an account is deleted, it is closed immediately and signed out everywhere. Your name, mobile number, email and PIN are removed and your flat is released. Financial records you were part of are kept, but they show "Former resident" and your flat number instead of your name. An apartment's last Admin must appoint another Admin before deleting their own account, so that the apartment is not left without one.

To remove an entire apartment and all of its records, the apartment's Admin should contact us using the address below.
