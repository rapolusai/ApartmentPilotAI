Delete your ApartmentCare account

For residents who no longer have the app installed

Fastest way: in the app

Open ApartmentCare, go to More → your profile → Delete my account and confirm with your PIN. The account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Fill in this form. We check that the request comes from the account holder (usually by calling the mobile number back or confirming with your apartment's Admin) and then delete the account. You will not be asked for your PIN.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Removed: your name, mobile number, email and PIN; your sign-in on every device; your claim on the flat.
  • Kept by the apartment: bills, payments, receipts and expense records you were part of, shown as "Former resident" with the flat number, because they are the association's accounts.

Details are in the privacy policy. If you cannot use this form, write to {{CONTACT}} from the email address on your account, or ask your apartment's Admin.
